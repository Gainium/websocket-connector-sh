# 019 — a Hyperliquid stream open refused at the cap is never retried

Bug spec (SKILL.md Part 0). Scope: `src/utils/hyperliquidBalancer.ts`
(balancer-mode routing of Hyperliquid `open stream` / `close stream`).

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

An account's Hyperliquid user stream opens as soon as some worker has room,
even if no worker had room when the open first arrived. Room appears when:

- **§1.1a** a worker restarts (its users are gone, possibly with a higher cap);
- **§1.1b** a worker's published cap rises without a restart (late IPs, 9355bd9).

### §1.2 Actual

`routeOpen` tries every alive worker. If all of them refuse (`reason: 'cap'`),
or the balancer's own load view says all are full, it logs
`open <uuid> dropped …` and returns. Nothing keeps the payload. The comment
in `route()` says the open is "left for main-app to retry", but main-app
does not retry:

- the bot sends `open stream` fire-and-forget, once per bot load or credential
  change, and never sees the worker's refusal;
- the bot's liveness repair (`assessUserStreamLiveness`) watches the
  `userStreamInfo<uuid>` Redis channel. The fill-failsafe's `PING`s keep that
  channel alive whether or not any exchange stream exists, so a missing stream
  never looks deaf.

`resendTo` (worker restart) and `rebalanceFrom` (worker death) replay only
**assignments**, and a refused open never becomes one. So the account has no
order stream until a bot on it is restarted or edited. Fills during that time
are not booked live, and the follow-up orders for them are not placed.

## §2 Evidence

- Production after a host reboot: the HL worker booted before the network,
  detected 0 IPv4 → cap 10, and refused opens with
  `HL cap pre-check failed (have 10/10); rejecting <uuid>`. When the worker
  restarted with the full pool (cap 60), those accounts stayed without a
  stream: there was no `accepted open` / `Hyperliquid connected` for them. A
  grid buy on one of them filled on the venue about 13 minutes later and was
  still `NEW` in Gainium more than 25 minutes after that.
- Local repro, `test/hyperliquidBalancerRetry.test.ts`, real balancer with fake
  Redis/Rabbit: on HEAD the refused open is still unassigned after a worker
  restart with cap 60 (§2.1), after a cap rise without a restart (§2.1), and
  after a worker-side RPC refusal followed by a cap rise.
- Origin: latent. `2c76e1e` (balancer drop path) and `825e502` (the "left for
  main-app to retry" comment, 2026-06-24). No main-app re-send was ever added.

## §3 Assumption

Retrying in the balancer is enough. The worker reports every successful open
on `userStreamInfo<uuid>` as `Subscribed to user <uuid>`. The bot already
answers that message with its check-orders-after-reconnect, which books fills
it missed.

## §4 Requirements

- **§4.1** A refused open (every worker refused, or none had room) is kept per
  uuid. Only the latest payload is kept, so rotated credentials win.
- **§4.2** Every watchdog tick retries the kept opens through the normal
  `routeOpen` path (same worker selection, same "never open locally" rule).
  The pass stops at the first open that is refused again, so a full fleet
  costs at most one failed attempt per tick.
- **§4.3** A `close stream` for a kept uuid drops it. A closed account is never
  reopened.
- **§4.4** A successful route drops the uuid from the kept set.
- **§4.5** The tick log line reports how many opens are waiting.

## §5 Out of scope

- Kept opens are in memory. A balancer restart loses them, the same as any
  open that arrived while the balancer was down.
- Late-IP detection itself is in 9355bd9.
