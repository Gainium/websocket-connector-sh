# 021 — a Kraken Futures private socket reconnects but never re-authenticates

Bug spec (SKILL.md Part 0). Scope: `src/userStream.ts` (Kraken user stream:
`reconnected` / `open` / `authenticated` handlers) and the SDK it drives,
`@siebly/kraken-api`.

## §1 Definition

### §1.1 Expected

After any reconnect of a Kraken Futures private socket (`derivativesPrivateV1`),
the client re-authenticates (challenge) and re-subscribes `open_orders`,
`balances` and `fills`, so order updates keep reaching bots.

### §1.2 Actual

If the previous socket closed while its challenge was still in flight, the next
socket opens, reports "reconnected", answers pings — and never authenticates or
subscribes again. No error is raised. The room looks healthy and is deaf until
the process restarts. Bots then learn about Futures fills only from their own
REST reconciliation, which cannot settle MARKET orders (exchange-connector-sh
§ for `getOrder`), so positions are left on the venue untracked.

## §2 Evidence

- Production user-stream log, during a Kraken Futures outage in which every
  private room cycled open → close dozens of times within minutes: after the
  venue recovered, rooms kept logging `Websocket reconnected` /
  `Kraken ws has reconnected`, but `Kraken ws authenticated` and
  `Kraken ws subscribed feed=…` stopped appearing for Futures entirely.
- Over the same span no `fills` or `open_orders` message was relayed for any
  Futures account, while Kraken spot `executions` kept flowing normally.
- A process restart restored `authenticated` + three `subscribed` lines per
  room immediately, and Futures order messages resumed.

## §3 Assumption (root cause, hypothesis)

`WsStore.rejectAllDeferredPromises` (SDK 1.1.3, unchanged in 1.1.8) skips the
reserved `AUTHENTICATION_IN_PROGRESS` promise. A close between challenge and
answer leaves that promise pending. On the next open, `onWsReadyForEvents` →
`assertIsAuthenticated` finds it and awaits it forever, so neither the auth
request nor the private resubscribe is ever sent.

The SDK emits `reconnected` synchronously inside `onWsOpen`, before
`onWsReadyForEvents`, so removing the stale promise in that handler lets the new
socket authenticate normally.

## §4 Requirements

- §4.1 On `reconnected`, a pending auth-in-progress promise from the previous
  socket is dropped before the SDK asks for authentication.
- §4.2 Removing, not rejecting: the old awaiter sits in the SDK's un-awaited
  open handler, where a rejection would be unhandled.
- §4.3 Defence in depth: a Futures private socket that is open for
  `KRAKEN_AUTH_WATCHDOG_MS` (default 30 s) without `authenticated` is closed and
  rebuilt through the existing self-heal path. A room already owned by the auth
  or transport breaker is left to that breaker.
- §4.4 Spot rooms are unchanged.
