# 019 — plan: retry Hyperliquid opens refused at the cap

Spec: `specs/019.hl-refused-open-never-retried.md`.

## Build order

1. Red: `test/hyperliquidBalancerRetry.test.ts`, which drives the real balancer
   (`route`, `tick`) over fake Redis/Rabbit with a fake worker that refuses at its cap.
2. `hyperliquidBalancer.ts`:
   - add `refusedOpens: Map<uuid, payload>`;
   - `routeOpen` keeps the payload on the drop path and deletes it on success;
   - `route()` close and `routeClose` delete it;
   - add `retryRefusedOpens()`, called at the end of `tick()`. It has a
     re-entrancy guard and stops at the first refusal.

## Contract decisions

- No new Rabbit/Redis keys or message shapes. The worker protocol is unchanged.
- The retry goes through `routeOpen`, so worker selection, the route mutex
  and the "balancer never opens HL locally" rule are unchanged.

## Blast Radius

- Balancer-mode user-stream process only (`userStream`). Workers and every
  non-HL exchange are untouched.
- Worst case: an account that dropped all its bots without sending a close
  gets its stream opened once room appears. That is the same outcome as if
  the first open had been accepted, and a later close removes it as usual.
- Cost while the fleet is full: at most one refused RPC per tick.
